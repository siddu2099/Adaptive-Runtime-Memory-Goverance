## 1. Executive Audit Summary

The manuscript draft (`manuscript/ieee_manuscript.md`, 290 lines) was audited sentence-by-sentence against the authoritative technical dossier (`ARMG_IEEE_Paper_Revision_Dossier.md`) and the repository implementation. 

### Key Findings:
1. **Critical Implementation Contradiction in Taxonomy**: Section 5 explicitly claims a 5-class taxonomy (`SYNTAX_ERROR`, `SCHEMA_VIOLATION`, `JOIN_ERROR`, `TYPE_MISMATCH`, `SEMANTIC_LOGIC`). The codebase (`agents/taxonomy.py` and `agents/error_diagnosis.py`) implements a canonical 7-tier exception taxonomy (`Validation`, `Syntax`, `Semantic`, `Planning`, `Permission`, `Resource`, `Execution`).
2. **Dataset Nature Representation**: Sections 1, 9, and 16 describe the data warehouse as an "enterprise warehouse" or "enterprise Star Schema" without explicitly clarifying that it is a synthetically generated benchmark created via `scripts/seed_warehouse.py` (seed=42, 2,000 facts, 4 tables).
3. **Safety Claims Scoping**: Sections 8 and 12 use language such as "guarantee safety in production data environments" and "100% pre-execution safety." While zero destructive statements reached PostgreSQL across 600 evaluated runs and 18 unit tests verified AST blocking, the manuscript must explicitly differentiate the implemented AST mechanism from empirically observed benchmark containment, avoiding claims of universal security.
4. **Replication & Sampling Interpretation**: Sections 9 and 10 cite 450 query runs across three seeds. Because greedy decoding (`temperature = 0.0`) is enforced and seeds are not forwarded to Ollama, these three runs represent isolated executions assessing pipeline reproducibility and memory-state consistency, not stochastic population variance ($n=3$ repeated runs, not $n=450$ independent random trials).
5. **Semantic Accuracy Ceiling**: The manuscript accurately reports the 68.00% relational execution accuracy for Mode 4 and Mode 2, but needs to more prominently emphasize that ARMG provides *zero* improvement in relational semantic equivalence over stateless self-correction due to the 7B model's reasoning boundary on complex window functions.
6. **Temporal Decay Status**: Section 6 and 14 describe continuous exponential decay ($\lambda = 0.05/\text{day}$). The audit verifies that while this is mathematically implemented and unit-tested in `memory/governance.py`, it was experimentally unexercised in the 3.5-minute benchmark run ($\Delta t \approx 0.002$ days) and must be explicitly identified as unexercised.
7. **Negative Constraints Generalization**: Section 14 attributes retry reduction in Mode 5 vs. Mode 4 to negative constraints, but must clarify that this empirical delta was isolated strictly to Query Q19.
8. **End-to-End Latency Overhead**: Section 10 reports the +19.79% latency penalty (8,564.89 ms vs. 7,149.68 ms), which must be framed consistently as an architectural trade-off throughout the abstract, introduction, and discussion.
9. **Citation Placeholders**: Pervasive `[REF]` placeholders require explicit cataloging into literature requirements without fabricating bibliographic metadata.
