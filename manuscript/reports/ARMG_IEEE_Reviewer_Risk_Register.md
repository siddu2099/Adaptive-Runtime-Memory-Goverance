## 2. Reviewer Defense Action Plan

1. **Front-Loaded Honesty**: By declaring the 68.00% accuracy plateau, the +19.79% latency penalty, the synthetic data scale, and unexercised temporal decay in the Abstract and Introduction, the manuscript preempts reviewer accusations of selective reporting.
2. **Defensible Systems Framing**: Position ARMG as an enterprise systems and governance architecture, emphasizing operational stability, token reduction, and AST safety rather than model-accuracy breakthroughs.
3. **Strict Statistical Rigor**: Treat $n=3$ repeated runs as pipeline reproducibility evaluations under greedy decoding, eliminating all statistical inference overreach.
